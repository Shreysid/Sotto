Local text-to-speech for macOS

Selected text,
spoken naturally.

Sotto turns text from any app into expressive speech on your Mac. No account, no subscription, no text sent to a cloud voice API.

Built for the way you already read.

Keep Sotto in the menu bar. Highlight a passage in a browser, editor, or chat app, then use your own global shortcut to hear it.

01

Expressive voice

Kokoro delivers a more human read than system text-to-speech, with local inference on Apple silicon.

02

Fast follow-up speech

Sotto warms the model after startup and begins long passages with a smaller first section for faster audio.

03

Your words stay local

Selected text and generated audio stay on your Mac. The network is only used for the first model download.

Small controls,
serious usefulness.

Change speed during playback, skip between sections, stop instantly, add pronunciation rules for names and acronyms, and let Sotto pause for voice apps.
